# The Money Codes — SEP Quick Reference

**What this is:** The codes that make you money during SEP season. Not all 37 — just the ones you'll actually use. Recognize what they say, know the code, close the enrollment.

---

## The #1 Off-Season Opportunity: C-SNP (Code: CSN)

17 million Medicare beneficiaries have a qualifying chronic condition. The CSN SEP is available year-round — no expiring window, no ticking clock. While other agents sit idle waiting for AEP, you're enrolling.

**The question that opens the door:** "Do you have any ongoing health conditions like diabetes, heart failure, or COPD?"

**Why this is the money code:**
- Year-round availability — no window expires
- 17 million eligible beneficiaries
- No time pressure on the call — take your time, explain the benefits
- C-SNPs offer lower copays and condition-specific care management
- Most agents don't even know about it

**Qualifying conditions:** Diabetes, chronic heart failure, COPD/emphysema, ESRD/dialysis, cancer, cardiovascular disorders, dementia/Alzheimer's, autoimmune disorders (lupus, RA, MS), HIV/AIDS, chronic mental health (schizophrenia, bipolar, major depression), neurological disorders (Parkinson's, epilepsy, ALS), end-stage liver disease, hematologic disorders, chronic alcohol/drug dependence, stroke/CVA

**Provider attestation:** The beneficiary's doctor must verify the diagnosis within 2 months of enrollment. Remind them to follow up with their provider.

**Key rule:** Must enroll INTO a C-SNP. Cannot use CSN for standard MA or MAPD plans. Verify a C-SNP exists in their county before confirming the SEP.

## "They Just Told You Something" — Here's Your Code

| What they said | Code | Window | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| "I have diabetes / COPD / heart failure..." | **CSN** | Year-round, 1x/yr per condition | Confirm diagnosis, verify C-SNP in their county |
| "I just retired / lost my job / COBRA ended" | **LEC** | Month of loss + 2 months | Get exact coverage end date. Forms: CMS-L564, CMS-40B |
| "I just moved" | **MOV** | Month before + month of + 2 after | Verify address change with Social Security |
| "I'm on Medicaid" | **INT** | Any month, repeatable | Verify full Medicaid → D-SNP. Check MCO match |
| "I get Extra Help / low income" | **DEP** | Any month, repeatable | Verify Medicaid or LIS level → PDP change |
| "I'm turning 65 / just got Medicare" | **IEP** | 7 months around 65th birthday | Check Part A and Part B effective dates |
| "My plan is terrible" | **5ST** | Dec 8 – Nov 30, 1x/yr | Check if a 5-star plan exists in their area |
| "I'm in a nursing home / rehab" | **OEP-I** | Unlimited while in facility | Confirm qualifying facility type (not assisted living) |
| "My plan got cancelled" | **EOC** | Dec 8 – end of Feb | Verify plan non-renewal notice from carrier |
| "I lost my VA / TRICARE / ACA coverage" | **LCC** | 2 months from loss or notification | Must be involuntary loss (not missed premiums) |
| "My Medicaid just changed" | **MCD** | 3 months from change | Gained, lost, or changed Medicaid level |

## "When Exactly Did That Happen?"

This is the single most important question you ask on every SEP call. Most codes have ticking clocks.

### Always Open — No Rush, But Enroll Today
- **CSN** — Year-round (as long as condition exists and C-SNP is available)
- **INT** — Any month, repeatable (full Medicaid → D-SNP)
- **DEP** — Any month, repeatable (any Medicaid/LIS → PDP)
- **OEP-I** — Unlimited while in qualifying facility
- **CDC** — Anytime (has other drug coverage, moving to MA-only)

### Ticking Clock — Get the Date, Do the Math
- **IEP** — 7 months around 65th birthday (3 before, birthday month, 3 after)
- **LEC** — Month of coverage loss + 2 months
- **MOV** — Month before move + month of move + 2 months after
- **MCD / NLS** — 3 months from Medicaid/LIS status change
- **LCC** — 2 months from loss or notification (whichever is later)

### Calendar-Bound — Know the Dates
- **5ST** — December 8 through November 30 (once per year)
- **EOC** — December 8 through end of February
- **AEP** — October 15 through December 7 (no SEP needed)
- **OEP** — January 1 through March 31 (existing MA members only, one change)

## The Confusion Killers

These are the code pairs agents mix up most. One question tells you which is which.

**IEP vs ICEP**
Same Part A and Part B effective date? → **IEP**
Different dates (Part B activating later)? → **ICEP**

**INT vs DEP**
Full Medicaid (income below $1,903/mo single)? → **INT** → D-SNP
Any Medicaid or LIS level? → **DEP** → PDP only

**MOV vs LEC**
Did they move? → **MOV**
Did they lose coverage? → **LEC**
(They might have done both — check for both)

**LCC vs LEC**
Employer/union/COBRA coverage? → **LEC**
Everything else (VA, TRICARE, ACA, other)? → **LCC**

**12G vs 12J**
Had Medigap before joining MA for the first time? → **12G**
Joined MA at 65, no prior Medigap? → **12J** (PDP mandatory)

## Three Lines You Never Cross

**1. DST is not a standalone SEP.**
A disaster alone does NOT create an enrollment window. DST only extends a window the beneficiary already had and missed because of the disaster. Never advertise it. Never bring it up. Carrier benchmark: ~15%. Exceed that and you get audited.

**2. Never enroll a Medicaid beneficiary in a give-back plan.**
Beneficiaries with Medicaid already have their Part B premium covered by the state. A give-back benefit has zero value for them and creates compliance risk.

**3. Always verify the election period with the beneficiary.**
Before every submission: What is the qualifying event? When did it happen? Does the beneficiary understand? This is a conversation, not a checkbox.
