# SEP Training Session -- Manager's Facilitator Guide

**Session length:** 2 hours
**Audience:** Licensed Medicare Advantage sales agents
**Goal:** Agents walk out knowing the money codes, recognizing triggers on calls, and understanding the compliance lines they cannot cross.

---

## Before the Session

### Materials Checklist

- [ ] Print the Money Codes quick-reference sheet -- one per agent
- [ ] Print the compliance cheat sheet -- one per agent
- [ ] Screen/projector set up with the Certainty System site loaded to `/sep-check`
- [ ] Whiteboard or flip chart for the "What Did They Say?" drill
- [ ] Optional: SEP Training Guide PDFs for agents who want the deep dive

### Room Setup

- Agents should be able to see the projected screen and their printed cheat sheets
- Keep it informal -- this is not a lecture, it's pattern recognition training

### Your Mindset Going In

This is not "here are 37 codes, memorize them." This is "here are the 5-6 codes that make you money, and here's how to recognize them on a live call." If agents leave knowing CSN, LEC, MOV, INT/DEP, and the three compliance lines they can't cross -- the session was a success.

## Hour 1: Foundation + The Money Codes

### 0:00 -- 0:10 | Why SEPs Matter (Talk)

**Key message:** AEP ends December 7th. From April 1st through October 14th, there is no open enrollment. If a beneficiary does not have a valid SEP, you cannot enroll them. Period. That means from April to October, your ability to make money depends entirely on whether you can identify SEPs on inbound calls.

**Say this:** "SEP season is where the agents who know their stuff separate themselves from everyone else. Most agents sit around waiting for AEP. You're not going to be most agents."

**Transition:** "Let me show you the codes that actually matter."

### 0:10 -- 0:25 | The Money Codes (Walk through the cheat sheet)

**Have agents look at their printed Money Codes sheet.**

Walk through each code in the "They Just Told You Something" table. For each one:

1. Read the "What they said" column out loud
2. Name the code
3. State the window
4. State the next step

Spend the most time on:

- **CSN** -- This is the #1 opportunity. 17 million eligible. Year-round. No ticking clock. The question that opens the door: "Do you have any ongoing health conditions like diabetes, heart failure, or COPD?"
- **LEC** -- Extremely common. People retiring, losing jobs, COBRA running out. Get the exact date.
- **INT/DEP** -- Medicaid population. INT = full Medicaid, enroll into D-SNP. DEP = any Medicaid/LIS, enroll into PDP. Both repeatable, any month.
- **MOV** -- People who moved. Verify address change with Social Security.

**Say this:** "You don't need to memorize 37 codes. You need to recognize what someone tells you on a call and match it to the right code. That's it. The cheat sheet does the rest."

### 0:25 -- 0:40 | "What Did They Say?" Drill (Interactive)

**This is the most important exercise in the session.**

Read each scenario below. Agents call out the SEP code. If they get it wrong, explain why. If they get it right, move on. Keep the pace fast.

**Scenarios (read these out loud):**

1. "I was just diagnosed with diabetes last month."
   **Answer:** CSN (verify C-SNP in their county)

2. "I retired from Ford two weeks ago and my benefits ended."
   **Answer:** LEC (month of loss + 2 months)

3. "I just moved from Ohio to Florida."
   **Answer:** MOV (verify address change)

4. "I'm on Medicaid."
   **Answer:** INT if full Medicaid, DEP if partial or LIS

5. "My husband passed away and I was on his employer insurance."
   **Answer:** LEC (death of covered spouse)

6. "I'm turning 65 next month."
   **Answer:** IEP (7-month window)

7. "I have COPD and I'm on oxygen."
   **Answer:** CSN (chronic lung disorder)

8. "I got a letter saying my plan is being discontinued."
   **Answer:** EOC (Dec 8 -- end of Feb)

9. "I'm in a skilled nursing facility recovering from hip surgery."
   **Answer:** OEP-I (verify facility type)

10. "My COBRA coverage just ran out."
    **Answer:** LEC (COBRA expiration counts)

11. "I lost my VA coverage."
    **Answer:** LCC (not employer = LCC, not LEC)

12. "I have heart failure and my doctor says I need a special plan."
    **Answer:** CSN (heart failure is qualifying)

**After the drill:** "Notice how you didn't need to memorize anything. You just listened to what they said and matched it. That's exactly what happens on a real call."

### 0:40 -- 0:55 | CSN Deep Dive (Talk + Live Demo)

**Pull up the Certainty System site on the projector. Go to `/sep-check`.**

**Key points to cover:**

- C-SNP is the single biggest enrollment opportunity outside AEP
- 17 million beneficiaries are eligible
- Available year-round -- no expiring window
- Must enroll INTO a C-SNP (not standard MA/MAPD)
- Provider must attest to the diagnosis within 2 months of enrollment
- C-SNP to C-SNP for the same condition does NOT qualify -- only a different condition

**Live demo:** Show agents how to use the SEP Check tool to verify CSN eligibility. Walk through a sample scenario.

**The question every agent should ask on every call:** "Do you have any ongoing health conditions your doctor treats regularly -- like diabetes, heart failure, or COPD?"

**Say this:** "If you ask this question on every inbound call from April through October, you will find CSN enrollments that other agents completely miss. This is how you eat during SEP season."

## Hour 2: Confusion Points + Compliance + Live Practice

### 1:00 -- 1:15 | Confusion Killers (Quiz Format)

**Have agents look at the Confusion Killers section of their cheat sheet.**

For each pair, ask the distinguishing question. Have agents answer.

**Quiz questions:**

1. "Beneficiary has Part A effective January 2024 and Part B effective May 2024. Same dates or different? What code?"
   **Answer:** Different dates. **ICEP**

2. "Beneficiary says they're on Medicaid. They get full benefits -- doctor visits, hospital, everything covered. INT or DEP?"
   **Answer:** Full Medicaid. **INT** -- enroll into D-SNP.

3. "Beneficiary says they get Extra Help with their prescriptions but doesn't have full Medicaid. INT or DEP?"
   **Answer:** LIS only. **DEP** -- enroll into PDP.

4. "Beneficiary moved from Texas to Arizona AND lost their employer coverage when they relocated. MOV or LEC?"
   **Answer:** Both! Check for both SEPs.

5. "Beneficiary lost their TRICARE coverage. LCC or LEC?"
   **Answer:** Not employer. **LCC**

6. "Beneficiary lost their employer insurance because they retired. LCC or LEC?"
   **Answer:** Employer. **LEC**

7. "Beneficiary dropped their Medigap Plan F last AEP to try Medicare Advantage for the first time. It's been 8 months. 12G or 12J?"
   **Answer:** Had Medigap. **12G**

### 1:15 -- 1:30 | "When Did That Happen?" (Walk through timing)

**Key message:** Most SEPs have ticking clocks. The exact date of the triggering event determines whether the window is still open. This is why "When exactly did that happen?" is the most important question on every SEP call.

Walk through the three timing categories on the cheat sheet:

- **Always open:** CSN, INT, DEP, OEP-I, CDC -- still enroll today, don't let them slip away
- **Ticking clock:** IEP, LEC, MOV, MCD, LCC -- get the date, do the math
- **Calendar-bound:** 5ST, EOC, AEP, OEP -- know the calendar

**Say this:** "If you can't get the exact date, you can't confirm the SEP. And if you can't confirm the SEP, you can't enroll. The date is everything."

### 1:30 -- 1:45 | Three Things That Get You Fired (Talk -- Compliance)

**This is non-negotiable. No jokes, no lightening the mood. This is serious.**

**1. DST is NOT a standalone SEP.**

A disaster does NOT create a new enrollment window. It only extends a window the beneficiary already had and missed because of the disaster. Never advertise it. Never bring it up. If a beneficiary mentions a disaster, ask: "Were you trying to make a plan change during the disaster and were unable to?" If not, DST does not apply. Carrier benchmark: ~15%. Exceed it and you get audited.

**2. Never enroll a Medicaid beneficiary in a give-back plan.**

Beneficiaries with Medicaid already have their Part B premium paid by the state. A give-back benefit -- which reduces the Part B premium -- has zero value for them. It creates compliance risk and wastes a plan benefit they can't use.

**3. Always verify the election period with the beneficiary.**

Before every submission, you must have a conversation -- not a checkbox -- confirming: What is the qualifying event? When did it happen? Does the beneficiary understand they are enrolling based on this event?

**Also mention the 5 most-watched SEPs and their benchmarks:**

| SEP Code | Benchmark |
|----------|-----------|
| DST      | ~15%      |
| MOV      | ~10%      |
| LCC      | ~4%       |
| ACC      | ~3%       |
| PAP      | ~5%       |

**Say this:** "Compliance is not optional. If you submit the wrong SEP, the enrollment gets rejected. If you do it repeatedly, you get suspended. If it looks intentional, you lose your contract. Use the compliance cheat sheet before every SEP enrollment."

**Show the `/sep-compliance` page on the projector.**

### 1:45 -- 1:55 | Live Scenario Roleplay

**You play the beneficiary. Pick 2-3 agents to handle the call.**

**Scenario 1 (CSN):**
You're a 72-year-old woman. You mention you've been managing your diabetes for 15 years and take insulin. You called because your current plan copays are too high. You don't know what a C-SNP is. The agent should identify CSN, ask about your county, check for a C-SNP, and explain the tailored benefits.

**Scenario 2 (LEC + ICEP):**
You're a 67-year-old man. You just retired last month. Your employer coverage ended. You've had Part A since 65 but delayed Part B -- it's activating now. The agent should identify LEC (employer loss) and ICEP (Part B activating with a different date), get the exact coverage end date, and mention the CMS-L564 and CMS-40B forms.

**Scenario 3 (MOV):**
You're a 70-year-old who just moved from Michigan to Florida. Your current plan doesn't have doctors in Florida. The agent should identify MOV, ask when you moved, and confirm you've updated your address with Social Security.

**After each roleplay:** Brief group feedback. What did the agent do well? What did they miss?

And you know the three lines that get you fired. Everything is on your cheat sheet and on the Certainty System site. SEP season starts April 1st. Be ready."
