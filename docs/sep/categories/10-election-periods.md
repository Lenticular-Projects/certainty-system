# Category 10: Election Periods

**Purpose:** These are not technically SEPs — they are standing enrollment periods available to all (or most) Medicare beneficiaries on a fixed annual schedule. But you must know them cold because they interact with every SEP you will encounter.

---

## The Annual Calendar

The Medicare enrollment calendar has three distinct seasons, and the rules change dramatically depending on which one you are in.

### AEP — Annual Election Period

**Code:** AEP

**When:** October 15th through December 7th.

**Who:** Every Medicare beneficiary in the country.

**What they can do:** Enroll in, switch, or drop an MA, MAPD, or PDP plan. No qualifying event needed.

**Effective date:** January 1st of the following year.

**Key rule:** The last plan submitted wins — if someone enrolls three times during AEP, only the final submission counts.

**Talk track:** "We are in open enrollment right now, which means we can look at all the plans available to you and get you enrolled today. Whatever we choose will take effect January 1st."

---

### OEP — MA Open Enrollment Period

**Code:** OEP

**When:** January 1st through March 31st.

**Who:** Beneficiaries who are ALREADY enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. NOT available to PDP-only or Original Medicare beneficiaries.

**What they can do:** Make ONE change — switch to a different MA plan, return to Original Medicare, or add a standalone PDP.

**Effective date:** 1st of the following month (January enrollment is effective February 1st, etc.).

**Key rules:**
- One change only — once used, it is gone until next year
- Can still be used even if the current plan has not yet effectuated (e.g., an AEP enrollment pending)
- MARx will flag when OEP has been used

**Important for dual-eligible beneficiaries:** Do NOT waste OEP on a dual-eligible beneficiary who has INT or DEP available. Those monthly SEPs are better options because OEP is one-and-done, while INT and DEP are repeatable every month.

**Talk track:** "Since you are already in a Medicare Advantage plan, you have the option to make one change during this open enrollment window. That change would take effect the first of next month."

---

### SEP Season — April 1 through October 14

This is not a formal enrollment period — it is the absence of one. Between MA OEP closing on March 31st and AEP opening on October 15th, the only way to enroll someone is with a valid Special Enrollment Period.

**Why it matters:** During these six-plus months, agents who understand all 37 SEP codes can still enroll beneficiaries year-round. Agents who only know AEP sit idle for 6 months.

**What to focus on during SEP season:**
- **Discovery mindset** — every call requires active listening for SEP triggers
- **Address mismatches** — check every caller's address against MARx (MOV)
- **Medicaid/LIS status** — ask on every call (INT/DEP)
- **Chronic conditions** — ask about ongoing health conditions (CSN)
- **Coverage changes** — listen for lost employer coverage, plan endings (LEC/LCC/EOC)
- **New-to-Medicare** — turning 65, delayed Part B, disability aging in (IEP/ICEP/IEP2)

## Standard Enrollment Periods Summary Table

| Period | When | Who | What They Can Do | Effective Date |
|--------|------|-----|-----------------|----------------|
| IEP | 7 months around 65th birthday | Newly eligible | Enroll in MAPD or PDP | 1st of eligibility month or 1st of following month |
| GEP | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | Missed IEP | Sign up for Part B; can enroll in MA/MAPD/PDP | Part B: 1st of month after sign-up |
| AEP | Oct 15 – Dec 7 | All beneficiaries | Join, switch, or drop MA/MAPD/PDP | January 1 of following year |
| MA OEP | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | Existing MA/MAPD only | One plan change | 1st of following month |
| OEP-N | Effectuation month + 2 | First-time MA/MAPD | One plan change | 1st of following month |
| Medigap OEP | 6 months after Part B (age 65+) | Enrolling in Part B at 65+ | Guaranteed issue Medigap | Varies |
| SEP Season | Apr 1 – Oct 14 | Everyone — with qualifying event | Requires valid SEP | Varies by SEP |

## CMS Election Period Hierarchy

When a beneficiary qualifies for more than one enrollment period, CMS expects you to use the one most beneficial to them:

1. **IEP/ICEP** — always takes priority
2. **MA OEP** — second priority
3. **SEP** — third priority
4. **AEP** — fourth priority
5. **OEP-Institutional** — when moving into, residing in, or moving out of an institution

Always use the enrollment period that gives the beneficiary the best effective date and the most plan options.
